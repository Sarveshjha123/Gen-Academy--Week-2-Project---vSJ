# AMER Renewal Addendum (FY27)
Applies to customers contracted in the United States and Canada. Overrides the global policies where they conflict.

## Currency
Contracts in USD. Canadian customers may be invoiced in CAD on request; Quebec customers may request a French-language contract.

## Notice period
Standard: **90 days** before contract end.
**US Public Sector: 120 days**, and renewals should align to the customer's fiscal year.

## Uplift caps
1-year: up to **7%**. Multi-year: up to **5% per year**. **US Public Sector: capped at 3%.**

## Approvals
Global matrix applies: AE up to 5%, RVP up to 10%, Deal Desk up to 20% off current ACV.

## Attrition threshold
In AMER, Deal Desk review is required only for downsells of **more than 15%** of current ACV (instead of 10%).
