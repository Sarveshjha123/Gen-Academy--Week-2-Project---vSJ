# AMER SPIFFs and Quota Programs (FY27 H2)

## Renew Early
Renewals closed-won **at least 60 days before contract end** retire **110% of renewal ACV** to quota (instead of the standard 50% flat / 100% uplift).

## Multi-Year Kicker
For a 3-year renewal, an extra **1.5% commission on the ACV of years 2 and 3**, paid at signing.

## Consumption True-Up SPIFF
**$1,000** for each consumption product (E–J) where overage is converted into a committed true-up at renewal.

## AI Attach SPIFF
**$1,500** per Product F attach on a renewal, minimum commitment of 500,000 credits per year. Stackable with Renew Early.
