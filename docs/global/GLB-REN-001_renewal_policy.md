# Global Renewal Policy (FY27)

Cloudpaw's fiscal year FY27 runs from February 1, 2026 to January 31, 2027.
Q1 = Feb–Apr 2026, Q2 = May–Jul 2026, Q3 = Aug–Oct 2026, Q4 = Nov 2026–Jan 2027.

## 1. Scope and precedence
This policy applies to every subscription renewal in all five operating units (OUs): AMER, EMEA, South Asia, LATAM and ANZ.
Where a regional renewal addendum conflicts with this policy, **the regional addendum takes precedence** for customers contracted in that OU.
Promotions cannot be stacked unless the promotion explicitly says it is stackable.
Any combination of renewal discount and promotion above **40% off list price requires CFO approval**.

## 2. Renewal timeline
- T-120 days: the AE creates the renewal opportunity.
- T-90 days: default notice date. **Price-increase notices must reach the customer by the notice date** set in the regional addendum (default 90 days).
- T-60 days: renewal proposal sent.
- T-30 days: unsigned renewals escalate to the RVP and Deal Desk.

## 3. Missed notice date
If the notice date has passed, the AE **cannot apply a price uplift unilaterally**. An uplift is only allowed with the customer's written agreement. Otherwise the renewal proceeds at the current price (auto-renewal contracts renew automatically at the current price).

## 4. Price uplift
- Default cap for a 1-year renewal: **7%** over current ACV.
- Default cap for multi-year renewals: **5% per year**.
- A flat renewal (0% uplift) for an Enterprise or Strategic customer requires RVP approval.
- Regional addenda may set different caps.

## 5. Auto-renewal and co-terming
Auto-renewal contracts renew for the same term at the current price unless either party gives notice within the notice window.
Add-ons bought mid-term are co-termed to the main contract end date and prorated.
