# Renewal Strategy Playbook

## Build every renewal plan in this order
1. **Check blockers:** notice date, legal requirements (DPA, GSTIN), required approvals.
2. **Read health and consumption:** find shelfware, overage and declining products.
3. **Set the base scenario:** flat or uplift within the regional cap (if notice was given in time).
4. **Add value:** true-up overage, expand products above 85% utilization, cross-sell products that are common in the customer's region, segment and industry (see the regional consumption benchmark).
5. **Protect against attrition:** an adoption plan for shelfware; right-size only under the attrition policy.
6. **Apply programs and promotions:** check eligibility and dates in the regional documents.
7. **Show the AE's outcome:** commission and quota retired for each scenario.

## Standard scenarios
- **A. Flat renewal:** lowest risk, lowest quota credit.
- **B. Right-size and true-up:** remove shelfware, convert overage; often ACV-neutral or positive.
- **C. Expansion and cross-sell:** B plus new products or seats; highest credit.
- **D. Multi-year:** lower annual uplift in exchange for a 2–3 year term.

## Handling a price-increase objection
Anchor on usage growth and value delivered, show the uplift is within the regional cap, offer a multi-year price lock instead of a discount, and never promise a discount above your approval level.
