# Attrition and Churn Policy (FY27)

## 1. Definitions
- **Downsell:** renewal ACV lower than current ACV (fewer seats or smaller commitment).
- **Partial churn:** the customer drops an entire product.
- **Full churn:** the customer does not renew.
- **Attrition** = ACV lost through downsell, partial churn or full churn.

## 2. Thresholds and reviews
- A downsell of **more than 10% of current ACV** requires Deal Desk review and a churn-risk note, unless a regional addendum sets a different threshold.
- Any partial or full churn must be logged with a reason code: PRICE, ADOPTION, COMPETITOR, BUDGET, M&A, PRODUCT_GAP, OTHER.
- Accounts with a **health score below 50**, or any account facing churn, must be registered with the **Save Desk** at least 60 days before contract end.

## 3. Right-sizing shelfware
Right-sizing (reducing seats or commitment) is allowed only when utilization of that product has been **below 50% for at least 3 consecutive months**.
- Up to **20%** reduction of a product's quantity at one renewal: AE can approve.
- More than 20%: RVP approval.
- Always offer an adoption plan before right-sizing.

## 4. Impact on the AE's quota and commission
- Attrition ACV is **deducted 100%** from the quota credit of that renewal.
- No commission is paid on lost ACV.
- Example: a $500,000 renewal that drops $40,000 of shelfware is booked as $460,000 renewal ACV, and the $40,000 attrition reduces the quota credit.
