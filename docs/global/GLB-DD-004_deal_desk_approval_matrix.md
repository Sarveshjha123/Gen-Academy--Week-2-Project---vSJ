# Deal Desk Approval Matrix (FY27, global default)

Regional addenda may override these limits. Discounts are measured **off the customer's current ACV**.

| Renewal discount off current ACV | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 5% | Account Executive (AE) |
| Up to 10% | Regional Vice President (RVP) |
| Up to 20% | Deal Desk |
| Up to 30% | VP Finance |
| Above 30%, or more than 40% off list combined | CFO |

## Payment terms
Standard: **Net 30**. Net 45: Deal Desk. Net 60 or longer: VP Finance.

## Non-standard terms
Termination for convenience, uncapped liability, custom SLAs and most-favored-customer clauses always need Legal and Deal Desk review.
