# Renewals Glossary
- **ACV:** annual contract value (committed, recurring).
- **TCV:** total contract value = ACV × years.
- **Uplift:** percentage price increase at renewal.
- **Flat renewal:** renewal at the same ACV.
- **Downsell / attrition:** ACV lost at renewal.
- **Shelfware:** licensed or committed capacity the customer is not using (utilization below 50%).
- **Overage:** consumption above the committed quantity.
- **True-up:** converting overage into a higher committed quantity at renewal.
- **Quota retirement:** the amount of a booking that counts toward the AE's quota.
- **SPIFF:** a short-term cash bonus for a specific sales behavior.
- **Save Desk:** the team that supports at-risk renewals.
- **OU:** operating unit (AMER, EMEA, South Asia, LATAM, ANZ).
