# ACV and Booking Policy (FY27)

## 1. What counts as ACV
**Annual Contract Value (ACV)** is the annualized, committed, recurring subscription value of a contract.
- Seat products (A, B, C, D): ACV = licensed seats × net price per seat per year.
- Consumption products (E, F, G, H, I, J): ACV = **committed annual quantity × net unit price**. Only the committed amount is ACV.
- One-time fees, professional services and training are **not** ACV.

## 2. Overage
Usage above the committed quantity is **overage**. Overage is billed monthly in arrears at **list price** (no discount) and is **not ACV** and does not retire quota.

## 3. Consumption true-up at renewal
At renewal, the AE may convert overage into a higher commitment (a **true-up**).
- The true-up quantity is based on the **trailing 3-month run rate × 12**, minus the current committed quantity.
- True-up ACV = true-up quantity × the renewal net unit price.
- A true-up is booked as **expansion ACV**.

## 4. Multi-year and ramp deals
- TCV = ACV × number of years.
- Ramp deals (price increasing each year): ACV for quota purposes is **year 1** ACV.

## 5. Booking requirements
A renewal can only be booked when: the order form is signed; regional legal requirements are met (for example DPA version in EMEA, GSTIN in India); and any required approvals are attached.
