# AE Commission and Quota Retirement Plan (FY27, global)

## 1. Commission rates (on first-year ACV)
| Booking type | Commission rate |
|---|---|
| New logo | 10% |
| Cross-sell (a product the customer does not own today) | 8% |
| Expansion (more seats or a larger commitment of an owned product, including consumption true-ups) | 7% |
| Renewal, flat portion (up to current ACV) | 3% |
| Renewal, uplift portion | 8% |
| Attrition (lost ACV) | 0% |

## 2. Quota retirement
| Booking type | Quota retired |
|---|---|
| New logo, cross-sell, expansion | 100% of first-year ACV |
| Renewal, flat portion | 50% of renewal ACV |
| Renewal, uplift portion | 100% of the uplift |
| Attrition | **minus 100%** of the lost ACV |

Multi-year deals retire only year-1 ACV unless a regional program says otherwise.

## 3. Accelerators (based on FY27 attainment to date)
- Below 100%: standard rates.
- **100–125%: incremental bookings are paid at 1.5x** the commission rate.
- **Above 125%: 2x**.

## 4. Regional programs and SPIFFs
Set by each OU in its sales program document. Paid quarterly; claim in the CRM within 30 days of close. Programs stack only when the program document says so.

## 5. Clawback
If a customer cancels within 90 days of signing, commission and program payouts are clawed back.
