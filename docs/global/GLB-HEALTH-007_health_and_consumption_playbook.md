# Customer Health and Consumption Playbook

## 1. Health score (0–100)
Health score = 40% utilization + 20% trend + 15% support + 15% executive sponsor + 10% product breadth.
- **Utilization (40%):** average 3-month utilization across owned products, each capped at 100.
- **Trend (20%):** per product, compare the last 3 months with the 3 months before. Up more than 5% = 100; within ±5% = 70; down more than 5% = 30. Average across products.
- **Support (15%):** open priority-1 cases: none = 100; one = 60; two or more = 20.
- **Executive sponsor (15%):** unchanged = 100; changed in the last 6 months = 30.
- **Breadth (10%):** products owned ÷ 4 × 100 (4 or more products = 100).

## 2. Health bands
- **Green (75–100):** renewal likely. Focus on expansion.
- **Amber (50–74):** fix the weak driver before the proposal.
- **Red (below 50):** register with the Save Desk (GLB-ATTR-003). Lead with value, not price.

## 3. Utilization bands and plays (per product, last 3 months)
| Utilization | Signal | Play |
|---|---|---|
| Below 50% | Shelfware, attrition risk | Adoption plan first; right-size only under the attrition policy |
| 50–84% | Healthy | Protect at renewal |
| 85–100% | Expansion-ready | Offer more seats or a larger commitment |
| Above 100% (consumption only) | Overage | Convert the overage into a committed true-up (expansion ACV) |

## 4. Per-user consumption
A rising per-user rate with flat active users means deeper adoption: a good sign for uplift and expansion. A falling per-user rate is an early warning, even when utilization still looks healthy.
