# South Asia Renewal Addendum (FY27)
Overrides the global policies where they conflict.

## Currency and tax
India contracts in INR; Sri Lanka and Bangladesh in USD. India: **GST at 18%** on every invoice, and the customer's **GSTIN must be on the order form before booking**.

## Notice period
Standard: **60 days**.

## Uplift caps
1-year: up to **8%**. Multi-year: up to **6% per year**.

## Approvals (South Asia override)
**AE up to 7%, RVP up to 15%, Deal Desk up to 25%** off current ACV.

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms are **Net 45** (replaces the global Net 30).
