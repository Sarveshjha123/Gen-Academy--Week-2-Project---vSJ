# South Asia SPIFFs and Quota Programs (FY27 H2)

## Seat Expansion Accelerator
When renewal ACV grows **more than 15%** over current ACV, commission on the growth **above 15%** is paid at **1.25x** the normal rate.

## Renew Early
Renewals closed-won **at least 45 days before contract end** retire **110% of renewal ACV** to quota.

## Partner Co-sell Bonus
An extra **1% commission** on renewals involving a registered systems-integrator partner.
