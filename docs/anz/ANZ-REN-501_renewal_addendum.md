# ANZ Renewal Addendum (FY27)
Overrides the global policies where they conflict.

## Currency and tax
AUD or NZD. GST: 10% Australia, 15% New Zealand.

## Notice period
Standard: **90 days**.

## Uplift caps
1-year: up to **6%**. Multi-year: up to **4% per year**.

## Approvals (ANZ override)
AE up to 5%, RVP up to 10%, **Deal Desk up to 18%**; above 18% goes to VP Finance.

## Government customers
Government customers must be on the **in-country data residency option** (AU or NZ hosting) at renewal. There is no extra charge.
