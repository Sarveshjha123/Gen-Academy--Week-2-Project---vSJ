# LATAM Renewal Addendum (FY27)
Overrides the global policies where they conflict.

## Currency
Contracts in USD. Invoicing in BRL or MXN is allowed with an FX clause (rate set on invoice date). Brazil requires a Nota Fiscal. Argentina: offshore USD payments need Finance review.

## Notice period
Standard: **60 days**.

## Uplift caps
1-year: up to **10% only with a written FX or inflation justification** attached to the opportunity; **6%** without it. Multi-year: up to **6% per year**.

## Approvals
Global matrix applies: AE up to 5%, RVP up to 10%, Deal Desk up to 20%.
