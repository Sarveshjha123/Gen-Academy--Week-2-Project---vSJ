# LATAM SPIFFs and Quota Programs (FY27 H2)

## Logo Retention SPIFF
**$1,000** for each at-risk account (health score below 50 at T-90) that renews at flat or higher ACV.

## Multi-Year Kicker
For a 3-year renewal, an extra **1% commission on the ACV of years 2 and 3**.

## Note
LATAM does **not** run a Renew Early program in FY27.
