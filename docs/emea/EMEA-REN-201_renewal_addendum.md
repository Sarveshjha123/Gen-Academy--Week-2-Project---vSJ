# EMEA Renewal Addendum (FY27)
Overrides the global policies where they conflict.

## Currency
EUR, GBP or CHF. UAE customers may be invoiced in USD.

## Notice period
Standard: **90 days**. **DACH customers (Germany, Austria, Switzerland): 120 days.**

## Uplift caps
1-year: up to **5%** (or up to **6%** if the contract has an inflation-indexation clause). Multi-year: up to **4% per year**.

## Approvals (EMEA override)
AE up to 5%, **RVP up to 12%**, Deal Desk up to 20% off current ACV.

## Data protection
Every EMEA renewal must be on the current **Data Processing Agreement, DPA v4.2**. Customers on an older version must re-sign **before the renewal can be booked**.

## Product F in Germany and France
Adding Product F (AI Agents) for customers in Germany or France requires the customer's works council acknowledgement form.
