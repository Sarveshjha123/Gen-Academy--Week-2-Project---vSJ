# EMEA SPIFFs and Quota Programs (FY27 H2)

## Cross-Sell Champion
A product the customer does not own today, added at renewal, retires **200% of its first-year ACV** to quota.

## Renew Early
Renewals closed-won **at least 60 days before contract end** retire **105% of renewal ACV** to quota.

## Note
EMEA does **not** run an AI Attach SPIFF or a Consumption True-Up SPIFF in FY27.
